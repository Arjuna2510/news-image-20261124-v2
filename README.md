A one-page newspaper front page summing up the world's news of
**Tuesday 24 November 2026** — rebuilt from the day's news so far, after
the v1 entry was built before most of the day's news existed.

Lead stories: overnight Russian strikes on Ukraine's energy grid, the
Abu Dhabi peace talks ending without a breakthrough, and Jensen Huang
walking back into Nvidia HQ. Every story on the page happened, or was
first reported, on 24 November 2026; the DATE CHECK box names the big
23 November and earlier stories deliberately left out.

`generate.py` draws `entry.png` (1024×1024) from the three original
photographic lead illustrations in `assets/`; `main.py` copies it to
the output directory. Run `python3 generate.py` to re-render.
